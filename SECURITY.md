## Bao cao lo hong

**Khong** mo public issue cho lo hong bao mat.

Thay vao do, dung [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/PhuccNguyen/randomapp/security/advisories/new)
hoac lien he truc tiep chu repo.

Vui long kem theo:

- Mo ta lo hong va muc do anh huong
- Cac buoc tai hien
- Phien ban bi anh huong

Chung toi se phan hoi trong vong 72 gio.

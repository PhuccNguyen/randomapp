# Code of Conduct

## Cam ket

Chung toi cam ket tao ra moi truong tham gia khong quay roi cho tat ca moi nguoi,
bat ke kinh nghiem, gioi tinh, khuyet tat, ngoai hinh, chung toc hay ton giao.

## Chuan muc

Hanh vi duoc khuyen khich:

- Su dung ngon ngu than thien va bao dung
- Ton trong quan diem va trai nghiem khac biet
- Tiep nhan gop y mot cach xay dung

Hanh vi khong chap nhan:

- Ngon ngu hoac hinh anh mang tinh xuc pham
- Cong kich ca nhan hoac chinh tri
- Quay roi cong khai hoac rieng tu

## Thuc thi

Bao cao vi pham qua issue hoac lien he truc tiep chu repo.
Moi khieu nai se duoc xem xet va phan hoi.

Dua tren [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) v2.1.
